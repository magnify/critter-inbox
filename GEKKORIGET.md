# Gekkoriget — sourced facts

Fetched 2026-09-10. Every line below carries the URL it came from. Where I
couldn't confirm something, it's in "Not confirmed", clearly separated, and
not used to fill in the sections above it.

**Anchor note, read this first.** The domain (`gekkoriget.dk`) is *not* the
authoritative source here — it currently carries no shop content at all (see
§1). The facts below are anchored on two independent third-party sources
instead: the Reptilmesser exhibitor list and a local news syndication

from both, but I could not read its content directly (Facebook serves a
login wall to unauthenticated fetches). Nothing here is inherited from the
domain's authority — the domain has none yet.

## 1. Name, site, location, shop type

- Spelling: **Gekkoriget** (one word, Danish). Confirmed by the domain name,
  the Facebook page name, and the Reptilmesser exhibitor list.
- Website: `gekkoriget.dk` resolves (HTTP 200, WordPress). But as fetched
  2026-09-10, the entire site is the **unmodified WordPress "Études" demo
  theme**, in German, describing a fictional architecture firm ("Études ist
  ein bahnbrechendes Unternehmen...") plus one placeholder "Hello world!"
  post dated 2024-10-21. There is no pet-shop content, no Danish text, no
  products, no contact page beyond template placeholders (Facebook/
  Instagram/Twitter footer links point nowhere — `href="#"`).
  Source: https://gekkoriget.dk/ — fetched 2026-09-10 (raw HTML, not the
  page-summary tool — see note below).
- City/address: **not found anywhere.** No source states where Gekkoriget is
  based.
- Physical shop, webshop, or both: **no evidence of either.** Every
  confirmed source shows Gekkoriget appearing as an exhibitor/breeder at
  reptile fairs, not running a storefront or a webshop with listings.
  Sources: https://reptilmesser.dk/udstillerliste-roedovre-61763/ (fetched
  2026-09-10); https://vores-aars.dk/a/gekkoriget-fremviser-eksotiske-arter-til-messe-i-nordjylland/b2ff7ff6-6679-473c-8567-807642d16e34
  (fetched 2026-09-10).

*Methodology note:* I fetched `gekkoriget.dk` twice — once through the
page-summary tool, once with a direct `curl` to get raw HTML. Both agreed:
the German architecture-demo content is really what's on the page, not a
tool error. Flagging this because it's the kind of thing worth
double-checking rather than trusting a single fetch.

feeder insects (crickets/mealworms/roaches), frozen mice, terrariums as a
retail product, or husbandry equipment. Gekkoriget reads, from what's
actually verifiable, as a **New Caledonian gecko specialist/breeder**, not a
general exotic-pet retailer. That's a specific claim worth flagging loudly
— see the "what changes the sample data" note at the end.

The exhibitor-list text has transcription quirks ("Chahoa", "Pheisumaer",
"bænkedere") — I've kept them as printed rather than silently correcting,
since I can't tell if they're the organiser's typos or shorthand.

## Not confirmed

Guesses, clearly separated from the above, none of them shipped as fact:

- **City/base of operations.** No source states one. Gekkoriget has shown
  at fairs in both Rødovre (Zealand, near Copenhagen) and Aars
  (Nordjylland), which if anything argues against guessing a single city —
  a reptile breeder plausibly travels the whole country for fairs. I'd
  leave this blank in the prototype rather than pick a city.
- **Whether a webshop is coming.** The domain is registered, live, and
  running WordPress with nothing built on it — reads like a site mid-setup
  rather than abandoned. Guess only, not fact.
- **Frogs as part of the range.** One AI-generated search summary mentioned
  "various frogs" alongside the gecko species. I went looking for this in
  every primary source I could fetch (exhibitor list, news article) and
  found no such mention in either. I'm treating that claim as unverified
  and probably a model-generated addition rather than something on a real
  page — excluded from §2 on purpose.
- **Facebook follower count / page category.** Same story — a number showed
  up in a search summary, not in anything I could read directly. Excluded.
- **Tone convention (du/De, emoji).** Danish small businesses and Danish
  Facebook pages overwhelmingly default to informal "du" — near-universal
  for this register. That's a convention, not something this site
  confirms; flagging it as the reasonable default for Scribe to write in,
  not a sourced fact about Gekkoriget specifically.
- **Currency/locale.** DKK and Danish are safe defaults for any Danish
  business regardless of what Gekkoriget's own pages say, since none of
  them say anything yet. Not Gekkoriget-specific; just the country default.
