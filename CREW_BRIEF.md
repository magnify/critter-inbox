# Brief

**The problem.** A small exotic pet shop gets flooded with customer messages
and the owner can't keep up. Most are routine: hours, stock, prices, "can you
hold one". A few are about a sick or struggling animal and need the owner
personally.

**The product.** An inbox that triages. AI drafts the routine replies so the
owner approves rather than writes. Anything that smells like an animal's health
gets no draft and goes to the top, flagged. The owner's job shrinks to reading
the ones that matter.

**Later.** Messages from more than one place (Messenger, Instagram, email, web
chat) in one inbox. Design the data so a conversation carries its source from
day one, even while there's only one.

**What we're optimising for.** Fewer minutes of the owner's attention per day,
without ever letting a health question through unflagged. Speed of reply is
secondary.

**Current state.** Single-file prototype with fake data. See `CREW_LOG.md`.
